About the Solution

A governed AI coordination layer for diagnostic report communication

CORE PRINCIPLE
“Fix the handoffs, not the clinician.”

The proposed layer improves coordination around reports — drafting, flagging, routing and tracking — without diagnosing, prescribing or making release decisions.

Brief case analysis

The current process relies on manual report handling, email-based reviewer coordination, repeated verification, sequential approvals and spreadsheet tracking. These disconnected handoffs create avoidable delays and weak end-to-end visibility.

ProblemFragmented handoffs
Root causeManual coordination

ControlHuman approval

What the market is reaching for

Landscape solutions concentrate on clinical analysis, report generation, QA or radiology/pathology workflow. The unifying opportunity is a workflow layer that carries a governed communication task across the last mile.

  • Reduce email and spreadsheet coordination
  • Surface exceptions to the appropriate reviewer
  • Preserve an explicit human sign-off gate
  • Create accountable routing and delivery records
